# Kamboja Village — Locations and characters

Status: proposed cast and settlement plan supporting [the main story](main-story.md). Arka and Kamboja are established names; all other names and character details below are suggestions.

## Village identity

Kamboja is a lived-in agricultural community at the edge of forest and mountain routes. It should feel worth returning to. The clinic, marketplace, learning house, and family homes give the spreading illness a visible effect on everyday life.

Organize the village around a central square, with the chief's hall and marketplace nearby. A residential lane connects Arka's home, the learning house, and the place of worship. The clinic should be easy to find from both the square and the entrance. Fields and livestock pens sit toward the outskirts, before the wilderness gate.

This is a narrative layout proposal, not a finished tile map. Keep essential opening conversations close together and allow players to visit optional locations in any order.

| Agricultural fields | **Jatmika**, farmer; **Dewi**, farmer and seed keeper | Grow food and preserve seed stocks | Show effects on food supply; provide early route information and optional supply tasks |
| Livestock pens | **Laras**, livestock keeper | Cares for goats, chickens, and other village animals | Establish the village's daily rhythm; offer a short optional act of help |
| Marketplace | **Ningsih**, general merchant; **Genta**, smith and repairer | Trade essentials and maintain tools and equipment | Supplies and equipment progression; changing dialogue as trade is disrupted and restored |

The chief's hall and gate support the required village leadership and departure scenes. Genta can work at a stall or small workshop beside the marketplace; a separate large smithy is not required for the first version.

Tama has a home and a school affiliation, but his scene location changes with story progress. Do not spawn duplicate copies of him at all three locations. The same rule applies to NPCs who move between work, home, and the ending celebration.

## Core character notes

### Arka — Protagonist

Arka begins with determination and personal stakes, not complete knowledge of the mainland. Through the journey, he learns to prepare, listen, and accept help. His combat strength should grow alongside his understanding of the community he is trying to save.

His defining promise is to return with the cure. Avoid replacing that motivation with an unrelated royal lineage or world-conquest plot unless a later story revision explicitly adopts it.

### Ratih — Mother

Warm, practical, and honest about the danger. She does not simply obstruct the adventure or disappear after granting permission. Her proposed recurring theme is that courage includes asking for help and knowing when to rest.

Suggested recurring beat: each return home reveals a small sign that she has been preparing for Arka's return—a repaired strap, food set aside, or news from a neighbor. These scenes should use ordinary human care rather than requiring a new magical family secret.

### Darma Wiratama — Village chief

Responsible and trusted, but unable to solve the crisis with village resources alone. His role is to offer knowledge, organize assistance, and explain the expedition's stakes. He does not claim expertise that belongs to healer Lestari.

He should acknowledge what is unknown about the routes and guardians. This makes his guidance useful without making him appear to have deliberately withheld an easy solution.

### Lestari — Healer

Careful, compassionate, and methodical. She explains why all seven ingredients matter and maintains confidence without promising that ordinary supplies can already cure the disease.

Her progress dialogue should name the ingredient Arka actually found and explain the next practical lead. At the end, she supports Arka's preparation of the cure rather than taking his final action away from him.

### Tama — Pupil and patient

An individual with interests beyond being ill: curious about maps and eager to hear about the outside world. Use one recurring question or drawing to connect the opening clinic scene with the ending school scene.

Keep the portrayal gentle. The player should care about helping him without being subjected to a hidden death timer or punishment for exploring.

## Village changes across the story

| Story phase | Visible changes | Dialogue focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Fewer stalls open, quieter lessons, clinic receiving patients | Uncertainty, neighbors helping each other |
| Ingredients 1–2 | A few signs of hope; supplies remain limited | Arka's first success and concern about the road ahead |
| Ingredients 3–4 | More volunteers supporting the clinic; some work remains unfinished | Shared effort and encouragement, without claiming the disease is cured |
| Ingredients 5–6 | Preparation space and distribution supplies readied | The community preparing for Arka's return |
| Seven ingredients collected | Ratih and Bayu welcome Arka; Darma organizes helpers | Urgency focused on completing the remedy |
| Ending | Market reopens, lessons resume, fields and pens become lively | Gratitude, recovery, and ordinary plans for tomorrow |

These changes follow story flags, not elapsed real time. NPC services necessary for progress remain available throughout the required route.

## Optional village quests

The [quest catalog](quest-flow.md) defines the full objectives, availability, and rewards for these one-time optional tasks:

| ID | Quest | Contact | Role in progression |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sq_kam_01` | A Stray by the Fence | Laras | Short, untimed animal-care choice near the opening route |
| `sq_kam_02` | Seeds for Tomorrow | Dewi and Jatmika | A nearby supply task available after permission |
| `sq_kam_03` | Hands at the Clinic | Bima | Short opening inventory interaction and recovery supplies |
| `sq_kam_04` | A Map for Sinta | Sinta or Mira | Record landmarks during normal mainland travel and return later |
| `sq_kam_05` | Stories for the Road | Wening, Sena, and Damar | A long-running relationship quest linking the three settlements |

Offer only the animal-care and clinic tasks as the initial short choices; do not require either before Arka can leave. These quests never supply a medicinal boss ingredient, unlock the mother's permission, or block the next main chapter.

Sena lives in Desa Tirta, while Damar belongs to Desa Wana Sela. See [mainland-settlements.md](mainland-settlements.md) for their locations. Optional visits and later dialogue connect those settlements to Kamboja while preserving its role as Arka's home and the final cure location.
